Privacy Policy

MTG eBay Repricer

MTG eBay Repricer is a personal software application used to manage and automatically update pricing for Magic: The Gathering listings on eBay.

Information We Access

The application may access information from an authorized eBay account through eBay's APIs. This information may include eBay listing, inventory, pricing, and account information necessary for the application to perform its intended functions.

How Information Is Used

Information accessed through eBay's APIs is used solely to operate the MTG eBay Repricer, including monitoring listings and updating listing prices according to the application's configuration.

Information Sharing

The application does not sell or share users' eBay account information with third parties.

Data Security

Access to eBay account information is protected using eBay's authentication mechanisms. Authentication credentials and tokens are intended to be stored securely and are not publicly displayed.

Contact

For questions regarding this application or this privacy policy, please contact the application owner through the GitHub repository.

This privacy policy may be updated as the application changes.
